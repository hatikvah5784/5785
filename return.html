At the moment when prophecy is being fulfilled,
a Chinese director, after five years of devoted effort,
seeking the origins of ancient civilizations and faith,
linking the historical threads between China and Israel,
tracing the footsteps of the Gospel’s journey to the East,
bearing witness to God’s love for China,
and revealing Israel’s calling
and mission in the plan of salvation.
