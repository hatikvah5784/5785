Ancient Path · Return to Faith

在預言實現的時刻，
一位中國導演以五年心血拍攝完成
尋訪上古文明與信仰之源，
連結中華與以色列的歷史脈絡，
探尋福音進入東方的足跡，
見證上帝對中國的愛，
揭示以色列在救恩計劃中的呼召與使命。

At the moment when prophecy is being fulfilled, a Chinese director, after five years of devoted effort, seeking the origins of ancient civilizations and faith, linking the historical threads between China and Israel, tracing the footsteps of the Gospel’s journey to the East, bearing witness to God’s love for China, and revealing Israel’s calling and mission in the plan of salvation.
